Create an environment (an EnvironmentTemplate plus an EphemeralEnvironment
that references it):

  base=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/petri-dev/petri-operator/main/config/samples
  kubectl apply -f $base/core_v1alpha1_environmenttemplate.yaml
  kubectl apply -f $base/core_v1alpha1_ephemeralenvironment.yaml
  kubectl get ephemeralenvironments -A

CRDs are installed once from the chart's crds/ directory. Per Helm's design, they are NOT touched by `helm upgrade` and NOT removed by `helm uninstall` - this protects your EphemeralEnvironments from being torn down on uninstall.
To update the CRD schemas when upgrading to a new release, apply that release's CRDs first:

  kubectl apply -f https://github.com/petri-dev/petri-operator/releases/download/<version>/crds.yaml
  helm upgrade petri oci://ghcr.io/petri-dev/charts/petri --version <version> -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}
